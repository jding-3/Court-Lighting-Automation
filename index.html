Operating Instructions

  • 1
    Sit on Bench (Idle)

    Clicking Sit on Bench places both players on the bench and starts the 10-minute inactivity timer.

  • 2
    1-Minute Warning Dim

    When inactivity finishes, lights dim to 30%. A notification appears for 3 seconds, while the live countdown continues on screen.

  • 3
    Movement Cancels Dim

    Dragging or moving either player immediately stops the dimming timer and restores full 100% lighting.

  • 4
    Scheduled Curfew

    At the configured auto-off time (outlined in red), lights turn off automatically. Dragging back restores active lighting.

Testing Tip: Use the green Fast-Forward to 1m Dim button to test the 60-second dimming sequence immediately without waiting.
